Amazon FBA Coffee Cost Calculator

This calculator helps you estimate the costs and profitability of selling coffee bags on Amazon FBA. Enter your product details to get an estimate.

Input Data

Parameter Input Description
Selling Price per Bag ($) The price you will sell each bag of coffee for on Amazon.
Production Cost per Bag ($) The cost to produce or acquire each bag of coffee.
Number of Bags in First Shipment The number of coffee bags in your initial shipment to Amazon FBA.
FBA Fulfillment Fee per Bag ($) The estimated fee Amazon charges for picking, packing, and shipping each bag. Use the Amazon FBA Revenue Calculator for a more precise value.
Box Length (in inches) The length of the shipping box.
Box Width (in inches) The width of the shipping box.
Box Height (in inches) The height of the shipping box.
Box Weight (lbs) The weight of the shipping box.
Monthly Seller Account Fee ($) Your monthly Amazon Professional Seller account fee.
Estimated Monthly Storage Fee ($) The estimated cost to store your inventory in Amazon's fulfillment centers for one month.
Shipping Cost to Amazon FBA ($) The cost to ship your entire initial shipment to Amazon.

Calculated Results

Parameter Formula Result
Amazon Referral Fee per Bag ($) Selling Price per Bag * 0.08 (if Selling Price <= $15), Selling Price per Bag * 0.15 (if Selling Price > $15)
Total Production Cost ($) Production Cost per Bag * Number of Bags in First Shipment
Total Amazon Referral Fees ($) Amazon Referral Fee per Bag * Number of Bags in First Shipment
Total Amazon FBA Fulfillment Fees ($) FBA Fulfillment Fee per Bag * Number of Bags in First Shipment
Total Revenue ($) Selling Price per Bag * Number of Bags in First Shipment
Total Estimated Costs (Excluding Shipping) ($) Total Production Cost + Total Amazon Referral Fees + Total Amazon FBA Fulfillment Fees + Monthly Seller Account Fee + Estimated Monthly Storage Fee
Total Estimated Costs (Including Shipping) ($) Total Production Cost + Total Amazon Referral Fees + Total Amazon FBA Fulfillment Fees + Monthly Seller Account Fee + Estimated Monthly Storage Fee + Shipping Cost to Amazon FBA
Estimated Gross Profit (Excluding Shipping) ($) Total Revenue - Total Estimated Costs (Excluding Shipping)
Estimated Gross Profit (Including Shipping) ($) Total Revenue - Total Estimated Costs (Including Shipping)
Gross Profit per Bag (Excluding Shipping) ($) Estimated Gross Profit (Excluding Shipping) / Number of Bags in First Shipment
Gross Profit per Bag (Including Shipping) ($) Estimated Gross Profit (Including Shipping) / Number of Bags in First Shipment

Important Notes:

  • FBA Fulfillment Fees: The FBA Fulfillment Fee is an estimate. Use the Amazon FBA Revenue Calculator in Seller Central for the most accurate fees based on your product's dimensions and weight.
  • Box Dimensions and Weight: Providing accurate box dimensions (length, width, height) and weight is crucial for precise FBA fee calculation. Amazon uses these to determine the size tier of your shipment, which directly impacts costs.
  • Shipping Costs: "Shipping Cost to Amazon FBA" is an estimate. Obtain actual shipping quotes from your chosen carrier.
  • Storage Fees: Storage fees can vary. Higher volumes and longer storage times increase these fees.
  • Referral Fees: The referral fee for coffee is 8% for items selling for $15.00 or less, and 15% for items above $15.00.
  • Other Costs: This calculator does not include potential costs such as advertising, returns processing, or long-term storage fees.
  • Seller Account Fee: The monthly seller account fee is pro-rated over the month. If you sell multiple shipments in a month, the cost per shipment will be lower.
